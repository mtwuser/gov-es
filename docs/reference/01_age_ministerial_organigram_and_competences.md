# AGE Ministerial Organigram, Public Policy Categorization & Core Statutes (`gov-es` — `E2/E3`)

> **Epistemic Credibility Grade: `E2/E3` (Statutory Organigram & Sectoral Regulatory Matrix)**
>
> This reference manual categorizes Spanish public information and mandatory regulatory frameworks by **Ministerial Competence Area (`Administración General del Estado — AGE`, `Ley 40/2015 LRJSP`)** and **Autonomous / Local Jurisdiction (`geo-es`)** so enterprise departments (`dept-iso-legal`, `dept-iso-finance`, `dept-iso-bio`, `dept-iso-eng`) and citizens can locate and apply the exact governing norm.

---

## 1. General Administrative Procedure & Public Sector Baseline (`LRJSP` & `LPACAP` — `E3`)

Every interaction with Spanish Public Administrations (`AGE`, `CCAA`, `EELL`, and Institutional Public Sector) is governed by the twin administrative laws of 2015:

- **`Ley 39/2015, de 1 de octubre` (`LPACAP`, `BOE-A-2015-10565` — *Ad Extra* Citizen/Enterprise Procedure)**: Governs electronic administrative registers, interested-party rights (`Art. 13`, `Art. 53`), mandatory electronic communication for legal entities (`Art. 14.2`), administrative silence (`Art. 24`), and administrative appeals (*alzada* y *reposición*).
- **`Ley 40/2015, de 1 de octubre` (`LRJSP`, `BOE-A-2015-10566` — *Ad Intra* Public Sector Organization)**: Governs the structural organigram of Ministries (*Ministros $\rightarrow$ Secretarías de Estado $\rightarrow$ Subsecretarías $\rightarrow$ Direcciones Generales $\rightarrow$ Subdirecciones Generales*, `Art. 54–67`), inter-administrative cooperation (`Art. 140–154`), and the National Subsidies Database (`BDNS`).

## 3. Territorial Competence Cross-Reference (`AGE <-> CCAA <-> EELL` via `geo-es`)

When applying any statute from `AREA-01..07`, cross-check the territorial code in `geo-es` (`ISO 3166-2:ES`):

1. **Fiscal Territorial Regimes (`AREA-01`)**:
   - **Territorio Común (`15 CCAA`)**: Governed directly by `AEAT` (`LGT 58/2003`, `IVA 37/1992`).
   - **Régimen Foral (`ES-PV` País Vasco & `ES-NC` Navarra — Disposición Adicional 1ª CE)**: Governed by *Concierto Económico* (`Ley 12/2002`) and *Convenio Económico* (`Ley 28/1990`) through the Provincial Treasuries (*Haciendas Forales* / *TicketBAI*).
   - **Régimen Económico y Fiscal de Canarias (`ES-CN` — `Ley 19/1994`)**: Applies `IGIC` and `AIEM` in place of mainland `IVA`.
2. **Environmental & Industrial Licensing (`AREA-04`, `AREA-05`)**:
   - State `Ley 7/2022` and `Ley 21/1992` establish basic legislation (`Art. 149.1.23ª CE`), while the Autonomous Community (`CCAA`) Environmental & Industrial Directorates grant operational permits (*Autorización Ambiental Integrada*) and Municipal Councils (`EELL`) grant urban activity licenses (*Licencia de Actividad*).
