# AGE Ministerial Organigram, Public Policy Categorization & Core Statutes (`gov-es` — `E2/E3`)

> **Epistemic Credibility Grade: `E2/E3` (Statutory Organigram, Administrative Cut-Points & Sectoral Regulatory Matrix)**
>
> This reference manual categorizes Spanish public administration rules, mandatory procedural cut-points (`Ley 39/2015`, `Ley 9/2017`), and sectoral frameworks by **Ministerial Competence Area (`Administración General del Estado — AGE`, `Ley 40/2015 LRJSP`)** and **Territorial Jurisdiction (`geo-es`)**, linking back to [01_constitutional_hierarchy_and_state_powers.md](../architecture/01_constitutional_hierarchy_and_state_powers.md).

## 1. General Administrative Procedure, Deadlines & Public Procurement Cut-Points (`LPACAP`, `LRJSP` & `LCSP`) [E3]

> **Exon [E3]:** *Every administrative procedure in Spain is governed by `Ley 39/2015` (`LPACAP`, ad extra) and `Ley 40/2015` (`LRJSP`, ad intra), while public procurement contracts follow strict quantitative thresholds (`N` vs. `N ± 1`) under `Ley 9/2017` (`LCSP`) ([01_constitutional_hierarchy_and_state_powers.md](../architecture/01_constitutional_hierarchy_and_state_powers.md)).*

All legal entities (*personas jurídicas*, `Art. 14.2 Ley 39/2015`) must interact electronically with Spanish Public Administrations (`DEHú`, `Sede Electrónica`, ` Cl@ve` / qualified `eIDAS` certificates) under the exact statutory deadlines and thresholds below:

| Statutory Parameter / Procedure | Governing Norm (`BOE`) | Exact Quantitative Cut-Point (`N` vs. `N ± 1`) | Legal Consequence on Expiry or Breach |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Default Day Computation (`Días Hábiles`)** | `Art. 30.2 Ley 39/2015` (`BOE-A-2015-10565`) | Excludes **Saturdays, Sundays, and declared holidays** unless a Law or EU norm expressly sets calendar days (*días naturales*). | Deadlines start on the business day **following** notification (`Art. 30.3`); if the last day is non-business, it extends to the next business day (`Art. 30.5`). |
| **Electronic Notification Acceptance Window** | `Art. 43.2 Ley 39/2015` | **`10` calendar days (`<= 10` days)** from availability in the Electronic Headquarters / `DEHú`. | At **`> 10` calendar days** without access, notification is legally deemed **rejected (`entendida por rechazada`)** and the procedure continues. |
| **Subsanation of Defective Applications** | `Art. 68.1 Ley 39/2015` | **`10` business days** (extendable by up to `+5` business days in non-competitive procedures). | Failure to cure within deadline triggers **withdrawal (`desistimiento`)** via express resolution (`Art. 21`). |
| **Maximum Resolution Deadline & Silence** | `Art. 21.2–3` & `Art. 24 Ley 39/2015` | Specific norm deadline (`<= 6` months unless Law/EU norm sets longer); **`3` months fallback** when silent. | General rule in party-initiated procedures: **positive silence (`silencio estimatorio`)**, except right of petition, public domain/service transfer, environmental damage activities, or appeals (`desestimatorio`). |
| **Administrative Appeals (`Alzada` / `Reposición`)** | `Art. 122` & `Art. 124 Ley 39/2015` | **`1` month** against express acts (`date-to-date`, `Art. 30.4`); open-ended from day after silence effects against presumed acts. | *Recurso de alzada* (`Art. 121`) is mandatory to exhaust the administrative channel (`agotar la vía administrativa`); *reposición* (`Art. 123`) is optional prior to contentious-administrative court (`LJCA 29/1998`). |
| **Minor Public Contracts (`Contratos Menores`, IVA excl.)** | `Art. 118.1 Ley 9/2017 LCSP` (`BOE-A-2017-12902`) | **Works (`Obras`) `< 40,000.00 EUR`**; **Supplies & Services (`Suministros / Servicios`) `< 15,000.00 EUR`**; max duration **`<= 1` year** (`0` extensions). | At **`>= 40,000.00 EUR` (works)** or **`>= 15,000.00 EUR` (supplies/services)**, minor award is unlawful; open/simplified tender procedure (`Art. 156–159 LCSP`) is mandatory. |

### 1.1. Internal Hierarchy of AGE Ministries (`Art. 54–67 Ley 40/2015 LRJSP`)

Within each Ministry of the `Administración General del Estado`, organs divide strictly into **Superior Organs** and **Directive Organs**:
- **Órganos Superiores (`Art. 55.2.a LRJSP`)**: **Ministros** (`Art. 61`) and **Secretarios de Estado** (`Art. 62`).
- **Órganos Directivos (`Art. 55.2.b LRJSP`)**: **Subsecretarios** (`Art. 63`), **Secretarios Generales** (assimilated in rank to Subsecretario), **Secretarios Generales Técnicos**, **Directores Generales** (`Art. 66`), and **Subdirectores Generales** (`Art. 67`).

## 3. Territorial Fiscal & Administrative Cross-Reference (`AGE <-> CCAA <-> EELL` via `geo-es`) [E3]

> **Exon [E3]:** *Always combine the substantive norm in `AREA-01..07` with the `ISO 3166-2:ES` territorial code from `geo-es` to distinguish Common Fiscal Territory (`15 CCAA`) from Foral (`ES-PV`, `ES-NC`), Canary Islands (`ES-CN`), and Ceuta/Melilla (`ES-CE`, `ES-ML`) regimes.*

When applying any statute from `AREA-01..07`, apply these deterministic territorial rules ([01_statutory_compliance_and_boe_navigation_guide.md](../guides/01_statutory_compliance_and_boe_navigation_guide.md)):

1. **Four Fiscal Territorial Regimes (`AREA-01`)**:
   - **Territorio Común (`15` mainland & Balearic `CCAA`)**: Managed by `AEAT` (`Ley 58/2003 LGT`, `IVA` `21% / 10% / 4%`, *SII* / *Veri\*factu*).
   - **Régimen Foral del País Vasco (`ES-PV` — `01` Araba, `20` Gipuzkoa, `48` Bizkaia; `Disp. Adic. 1ª CE` & `Ley 12/2002` Concierto Económico)**: Provincial *Haciendas Forales* levy agreed taxes and enforce real-time **TicketBAI / Batuz** invoicing.
   - **Régimen Foral de Navarra (`ES-NC` — `31` Navarra; `Ley 28/1990` Convenio Económico)**: Governed by *Hacienda Foral de Navarra* under its own *Ley Foral General Tributaria*.
   - **REF Canarias (`ES-CN` — `35` Las Palmas, `38` S.C. Tenerife; `Ley 19/1994` & `Ley 20/1991`)** and **Ceuta/Melilla (`ES-CE` `51`, `ES-ML` `52`; `Ley 8/1991`)**: Exempt from harmonized EU `IVA`; apply **`IGIC`** (`7%` general rate in Canarias) + `AIEM`, or **`IPSI`** in Ceuta and Melilla, with customs `DUA` documentation on physical goods shipments.
2. **Three-Level Industrial & Environmental Permitting (`AREA-04`, `AREA-05`)**:
   - **State (`AGE`)**: Enacts basic floor legislation (`Ley 7/2022`, `Ley 21/1992`, `RD 809/2021`).
   - **Autonomous Community (`CCAA`)**: Grants *Autorización Ambiental Integrada (`AAI`, `RDLeg 1/2016`)*, Waste Manager/Producer registration (`eSIR`), and *Registro Integrado Industrial (`RII`)*.
   - **Municipality (`Ayuntamiento`, `EELL`)**: Grants Urban Compatibility Report (*Informe Urbanístico*) and Municipal Activity / Opening License (*Licencia Ambiental o Declaración Responsable*).
